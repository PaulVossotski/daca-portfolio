# Tallinn Store — Data Story

**Role A · Week 6 (Data Storytelling)**

Tallinn is UrbanStyle's flagship and largest store, and it sets the pace for the
whole company. The dashboard shows steady sales that climb every autumn to a
sharp December 2024 peak of €58,977 — about €21K above the store's ~€37,835
monthly average — driven by holiday demand and a handful of hero products led by
the "Praktiline džersii seelik". But the same data reveals a blind spot: 40% of
Tallinn's revenue comes from guests with no loyalty tier, customers the company
can't yet recognise or re-target. The recommendation is to protect the December
engine (stock the top sellers early, repeat the holiday campaign) while launching
a loyalty push to convert that large anonymous segment into known, repeat buyers.
