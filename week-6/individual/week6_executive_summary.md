# Tallinn Store — Executive Summary

**Role A · Week 6** — every point passes the "So what?" test (finding, why it matters, action).

- **Tallinn is the flagship: €983,720 revenue, 3,415 orders, €288 average order.**
  So what? It's the biggest store and the benchmark — whatever wins here is worth
  copying to Tartu and Pärnu.
- **December is the engine: €58,977 in Dec 2024, ~€21K above the monthly average.**
  So what? The holiday spike is predictable — stock the top sellers early and
  repeat the December campaign rather than reacting late.
- **Revenue leans on a few hero products; "Praktiline džersii seelik" alone drove €9,902.**
  So what? A stockout on these would visibly dent the month — protect their
  inventory first.
- **40% of revenue comes from guests with no loyalty tier (1,786 unique customers total).**
  So what? Nearly half the money is from customers UrbanStyle can't yet re-target
  — a loyalty programme could convert a huge anonymous base into repeat buyers.

**One-line takeaway for Kristi:** Tallinn is healthy and seasonal — defend the
December peak and turn anonymous guests into members to unlock the next leg of growth.
